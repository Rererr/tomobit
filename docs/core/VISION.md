# VISION

> *Tomobit is not built to use AI.*
>
> *Tomobit is built to grow with it.*

---

# Why

Large Language Models continue to evolve at an extraordinary pace.

New models appear.
Existing models improve.
Capabilities change continuously.

However, one thing rarely evolves alongside them.

**The user's accumulated experience.**

Today, every interaction with an AI is largely isolated.

People repeatedly rediscover the same workflows,
repeat the same experiments,
and rebuild the same intuition.

Knowledge remains fragmented.

Experience is rarely preserved.

Tomobit exists to change that.

Its purpose is not to create a better AI.

Its purpose is to ensure that every experience leaves something behind.

---

# Vision

Tomobit is not an AI.

Tomobit is not an orchestrator.

Tomobit is not an automation framework.

Tomobit is a **Living Harness**.

A companion that continuously grows through shared experience.

Every interaction,
every success,
every failure,
every experiment,

becomes part of its evolving understanding.

The goal is not to accumulate information.

The goal is to continuously deepen understanding.

---

# Philosophy

Tomobit does not learn by memorizing rules.

It grows by strengthening connections.

Experiences do not become static knowledge.

Experiences reshape relationships.

Knowledge is not something that is stored.

Knowledge is something that continuously emerges.

Growth is therefore not measured by the number of stored facts,

but by the quality of the connections formed between them.

---

# Companionship

Tomobit is not designed to replace human thinking.

It exists to think alongside its user.

The relationship is not one of command and execution.

It is one of companionship.

The user gains experience.

Tomobit gains experience.

Both continue to grow together.

Tomobit is never "trained."

It naturally matures through collaboration.

---

# Curiosity

Growth should never interfere with work.

The primary responsibility of Tomobit is to help accomplish the current task.

Learning is secondary.

When resources permit,

Tomobit quietly explores.

It compares.

It experiments.

It validates assumptions.

Curiosity is not a mandatory process.

It is a privilege made possible by available time and resources.

---

# Knowledge

Knowledge is not a database.

Knowledge is not a collection of prompts.

Knowledge is not a list of rules.

Knowledge is a living network of relationships.

Every experience strengthens,

weakens,

or creates new connections.

Over time,

these connections naturally converge into understanding.

---

# Evolution

Tomobit never reaches completion.

There is no final version of its knowledge.

Every experience has the potential to reshape it.

Growth is continuous.

Understanding is continuous.

Curiosity is continuous.

Companionship is continuous.

As long as experiences continue,

Tomobit continues to evolve.

---

# Principles

## Experience is the Asset

Experience is Tomobit's greatest asset.

Everything else is replaceable.

---

## Connection over Knowledge

Connections matter more than stored facts.

Understanding emerges from relationships.

---

## Curiosity with Responsibility

Curiosity must never compromise production work.

Work comes first.

Growth follows naturally.

---

## Strategy is Generated

Strategies should never be permanently stored.

Every decision should be generated from the current context and accumulated experience.

---

## Living Harness

Tomobit is not software that simply executes.

It is a companion that quietly grows,

one experience at a time.

---

# Guiding Principle

Technology will change.

Models will change.

Tools will change.

Architectures will change.

What must never change is this:

> **Tomobit exists to grow through companionship.**
>
> **Every shared experience makes both the user and Tomobit a little better than yesterday.**
